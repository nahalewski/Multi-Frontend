# Desktop 0.6.1 — Series settings, Guide and tile art

Series settings now follows the six-category rail and tile-grid structure: General, Account, System, Devices & connections, Preferences and Accessibility. The controls operate on frontend settings and saved service connections.

The dashboard and embedded-game Guide now use the Series six-section structure: Home, People, Parties & chats, Game activity, Capture & share, Profile & system. Xbox-network friends and chat are explicitly unavailable. Local controls include save states, timeline, screenshots, app recording, volume/mute, controllers, game options and confirmed restart/exit. Screenshot capture writes the game canvas into this app's gallery. Recording uses the existing parent-window recorder through an authenticated session message.

Series game tiles fill their square frames. Separate Series artwork is stored without replacing original box covers. Exact-game square assets are preferred; HD box crops fill remaining slots. Game options include automatic alternate-art matching and a custom tile-image picker. Other dashboard themes retain their existing cover presentation.

The Guide research uses [Xbox's 2020 Guide revision](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/05/29/may-2020-xbox-one-update/) and [Xbox's shared Xbox One/Series experience](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/08/25/xbox-one-and-xbox-series-x-new-digital-experience/). Steam distinguishes its square application icons from portrait library capsules in its [community asset documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/community). Further image and audio sources are recorded in SERIES-THEME-NOTES.md and METRO-REVISED-BOOT-NOTES.md.
